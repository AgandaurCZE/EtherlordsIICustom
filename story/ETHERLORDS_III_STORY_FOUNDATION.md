# Etherlords III — Story Foundation and Open Finale

Status: **working story canon / planning notes**, agreed in conversation on 2026-11-21. This is a narrative reference, not an implemented campaign or a claim that new details are established Etherlords I/II canon.

## Core premise

**Working title:** *Etherlords III: The Empty Throne* (the earlier project subtitle *Echoes of the Ether* also exists; final title not decided).

**Time:** Approximately **15 years after Etherlords II** and Diamanda's death.

The White Lord is gone. The four races remain deeply divided, geographically isolated, and bound to their respective Ether currents. Etherworld is recovering from the Pale Plague, but White Ether is becoming unstable and disturbances are spreading. Each faction sees a different answer to the resulting crisis, and each ultimately seeks control, even when it uses the language of preservation or liberation.

The campaign must respect the original series' biological and geographical divisions: Kinets (Blue; snowy, icy lands and winged humanoids), Vitals (Green; forests and life), Synthets (Black; desert, machinery, augmentation and necromancy), and Chaots (Red; volcanic/rocky regions, martial clans and great beasts). Alliances between factions are exceptional and unstable. **Do not posit ordinary biological Kinet/Vital offspring** or casual cross-current mastery. White Ether/multiple-current users must remain extreme anomalies with a specific cause.

The specific mechanism by which Diamanda died and how her actions changed White Ether is still a lore detail to verify; avoid treating speculative explanations as established game canon.

## Player routes and scope

| Storyline | First faction | Second faction | Finale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Preservation** (informally the “Good” side) | **Kinets** | **Vitals** | A White finale centered on **Vaelir** |
| **Dominion** (informally the “Evil” side) | **Synthets** | **Chaots** | A White finale centered on an **engineered counterpart** |

There are **4 missions per faction**, so each route consists of 8 faction missions plus an intended 4-mission finale (**12 missions per playthrough**). Four faction chapters require **16 mission designs**. The White endgame may have separate maps/scripts for the two paths: if fully separate, the total would be **24 mission designs** (16 + 8), versus **20 core designs** if four finale layouts are shared with variants. **Neither implementation nor exact native White branch allocation is settled.** Investigate the original White campaign configuration before locking the map count or which stages are shared.

“Good” and “Evil” are shorthand only. The story should avoid portraying either alliance as morally unambiguous.

## Returning characters

- **Mirabell (Kinets):** Former young Kinet protagonist, now older and experienced. Respected but potentially inconvenient to the Kinet establishment. Involved in investigating Ether instability and helping the young Vaelir restore his Blue affinity. Exact office/status remains open.
- **Eileen (Vitals):** A member of the **Vital Council of Elders**; intelligent, self-assured, scholarly, sometimes patronizing or overly certain of her judgment. She and Mirabell can disagree strongly about how to protect people versus protect the balance. Not currently planned as the playable Vital protagonist.
- **Alpha-100F / Steel (Synthets):** The former protagonist whose consciousness or remaining self has become part of a great Synthet machine built amid the long-term consequences of Pale devastation. Its final cycle and revelations are central to the Synthet story. Whether Alpha survives as an independent person is open.
- **Konnar (Chaots):** Fifteen years on, now the powerful ruler / dominant leader of the Chaots, having consolidated most clans by strength. The playable Chaot protagonist. He has political responsibilities beyond winning individual battles, and his relationship with the Synthet creation need not be simple master-and-weapon.

These are proposed events for Etherlords III, not claims about the post-game status of the characters in Etherlords II.

## Synthet crisis and the machine

A massive Synthet machine, linked to Alpha-100F's consciousness, was built to prevent another existential catastrophe and stabilize the homeland's Ether systems. It requires a severe sacrifice and is approaching the end of a cycle. **At the conclusion of the Synthet campaign, it produces detailed information that allows Synthet engineers to create an optimized cybernetic being**: mostly machinery, with a small number of human-like biological organs necessary to live and function in society.

The machine has gathered data on the rare Ether abnormality associated with Vaelir, which explains the new being's **direct, intentional likeness to Vaelir**. The exact method for obtaining a precise appearance/body blueprint is unresolved and must be established credibly (for example, observation, a projection or a particular data connection), rather than magic omniscience. The created being is not Vaelir captured/transformed and is **not a repeat of Diamanda's Synthet experimentation**.

## White protagonist A — Vaelir (Preservation)

- **Name:** Vaelir (working name agreed as suitable).
- **Age:** approximately **18** at story start; born about **three years before Etherlords II**.
- **Biological race:** **Kinet**, with two Kinet parents. Winged, humanoid. **Not** a Kinet/Vital biological hybrid; not Diamanda's child or reincarnation.
- **Origin:** While a small child during the Pale Plague, he was exposed to a severe Ether disturbance. His natural **Blue Ether connection was damaged**. Following Diamanda's death and the altered state of White Ether, this damage developed into an unusual *receptiveness* to other currents. The **interaction of both events**, rather than either alone, is our favored proposed cause.
- **Why White Lord missed him:** During the White Lord's lifetime Vaelir was a very young, apparently magic-impaired Kinet, **not an active multi-Ether wielder** and not a plausible threat.
- **Arc:** Mirabell/Kinet inquiry and later Eileen/Vital study and exposure help him **restore his Blue affinity**. In the Vital lands he gradually adapts to **Green Ether as well**, becoming an exceptional **dual-current Blue + Green user**. This is difficult, progressive, and never simply explained by race mixing or being “chosen.”
- **Appearance changes:** Initially Kinet-like, later grows **Vital roots / living plant patterns** or vegetation integrated into clothes, armor or wings. Tie visual stages to level breakpoints, reminiscent of Etherlords II hero evolutions. The precise transformation levels still need investigation.
- **Personal conflict:** Born free, initially unsure how to control his power; must learn whether his mentors wish to help him or use him to dictate Etherworld's future.

## White protagonist B — engineered counterpart (Dominion)

- **Name:** **Undecided.** May have a Synthet designation at creation and later choose a personal name as it grows independent. Do **not** name it Vaelir II as a final name.
- **Origin:** At the end of the Synthet campaign, Alpha-100F's machine finishes its cycle and provides a viable blueprint based on the Vaelir anomaly. Synthet engineers **construct** a distinct being to exploit multiple currents: mostly mechanical, with some biological organs. It resembles **Vaelir physically and facially** but has **black wings** and a more cybernetic body.
- **First Ether:** **Black (Synthet)**, by engineering and original design.
- **Second Ether:** **Red (Chaot)** develops during the Chaot portion / time in Chaot lands, through adaptation and improvements, not by magically becoming biologically Chaot. As it levels, it modifies/evolves its own form and develops a **red horn**, alongside further Black/Red design changes.
- **Visual progression:** Mechanical Kinet-like form with black wings → beginnings of Red Ether adaptation and red horn → visibly evolved Black/Red form. The design deliberately parallels but contrasts Vaelir's organic Blue/Green evolution.
- **Personal conflict:** Created to obey and initially powerful, but gradually gains identity, autonomy and a will of its own. Konnar can eventually respect its strength and self-determination. Avoid treating it as a mindless villain.

The two are **different beings existing in the same world**, not alternate universe versions of one person. Their nearly identical appearance is an intentional consequence of Synthet design that must be explained, not a coincidence. Their ability to channel two currents is exceptional and needs distinct causes.

## Finale — OPEN ENDING (agreed direction)

**Do not culminate in Vaelir fighting and killing the construct. Do not settle their ideological rivalry in Etherlords III.** The pair represent competing answers to the struggle for the Empty Throne and may *eventually* clash, but that confrontation is **teased, not depicted**. Both survive the ending and remain active in Etherworld.

The two White chapters each follow **their own dual-Ether protagonist**, maintaining their different allies, visual evolution, methods, and convictions. Both converge on **one overarching crisis at the Temple of Time / White Ether**, with a **different boss-like encounter** rather than a protagonist-versus-protagonist final fight. There could be **one underlying final boss/threat used in both routes** and viewed from different perspectives. Its identity and mechanics are **not decided**: avoid defaulting automatically to an evil Alpha-100F or a reincarnated White Lord.

**Asymmetric timing:** One protagonist **reaches the critical location first**, depending on the route being played, giving distinct entry dialogue, environmental conditions or objective order. The other may arrive later, be glimpsed, or leave evidence of their passage. Keep the two routes narratively consistent as a single world; do not casually make the same event happen contradictorily in two canon timelines. The exact chronology and whether the non-played route's hero is present need later planning.

**Ending beat:** The immediate shared catastrophe is contained or dealt with, yet the deeper question of who should control White Ether / the Empty Throne remains unresolved. The finale hints that the two dual-wielders' opposing ideals will bring them into confrontation **one day**. End on a restrained tease/cliffhanger for a hypothetical DLC or future Etherlords game, **without promising that expansion will actually exist**.

Avoid earlier pitches that definitively close the story with “Destroy the White Lord system forever” on one path and “Become the Fifth Sovereign” on the other. Such outcomes are not the chosen ending anymore. The finales can differ in sacrifices, companions and interpretation of success while preserving **both characters alive and their ideological conflict unresolved**.

## Guiding narrative contrast

> **Vaelir:** Born free, learns to command power and to live alongside more than one Ether current.
>
> **Engineered counterpart:** Created to serve, learns to become free, adapt its body and choose its own purpose.
>
> They do not fight in Etherlords III. **The possibility of their future clash is the final promise—not the final battle.**

## Agreed narrative pattern for the four faction campaigns (2026-11-21)

Each faction campaign is a self-contained four-mission adventure with its **own crisis, meaningful resolution, character decisions, and optional exploration**. Do not reduce it to a four-mission introduction for the later White hero.

- **Missions 1–3:** The current faction confronts escalating regional problems, undertakes quests and makes decisions within its own lands.
- **Mission 4:** Resolve that faction's immediate central conflict **and** present one brief, significant encounter, observation or event involving its route's White protagonist, to be revisited/explained in the White campaign.
- **First faction on a route (Kinets / Synthets):** Discover an exceptional living anomaly (Vaelir) or develop the circumstances/blueprint of an exceptional engineered being (Construct), but keep important details hidden.
- **Second faction (Vitals / Chaots):** Observe the protagonist's **second Ether affinity** beginning to emerge, through a short encounter or its aftermath; foreshadow a particular White-campaign event without explaining all its causes.
- **White finale:** Play through the protagonist's earlier unseen journey and revisit the foreshadowed event from the inside. Reveal its real causes and consequences; do not merely repeat the same mission. Both White paths resolve the immediate shared crisis but leave the protagonists' eventual ideological confrontation unplayed.

Examples, not locked scenes: Mirabell sees Vaelir trigger a strange ancient Kinet structure; a later Vital witness sees Blue/Green power in a forest. Synthet machine completes a cycle and supplies Construct's blueprint; Konnar later witnesses its Black/Red evolution.

**Ordering principle:** Consequences may be shown before causes, but both protagonists always exist in the same timeline. Repeated locations or shared scenes should remain consistent across viewpoints.

## Provisional level-design and mission scope (not locked)

The official map editor reportedly supports 20×20 through 240×240 blocks. Proposed first-Kinet campaign pacing, to be playtested:

| Mission | Map | Enemy battle encounters | Main questlines | Side questlines |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| K1 — First Disturbance (working) | 40×40 | 8–10 | 2 | 2 |
| K2 — The Frozen Passage (working) | 56×56 | 12–15 | 3 | 3 |
| K3 — Echoes Beneath the Ice (working) | 72×72 | 16–20 | 3 | 4 |
| K4 — The Silent Current (working) | 80×80 | 18–22 | 4 | 4 |

These counts are **planning suggestions**, not game limits or finalized content. A few stronger optional encounters and a distinct climax per mission are desirable. Enemy encounters do not count the cards summoned within a battle. Individual questlines can contain multiple tasks.

**Level targets require verification** of the actual cap and XP curve. A provisional overall cap of level 20 was discussed, but a more sustainable plan will likely use different starting levels for each faction hero and Vaelir rather than assuming a single continuous hero across faction changes. Preserve native progression within each hero's chapter. Test hero appearance breakpoints and level 20 explicitly.

## Correction: actual map-editor sizes (2026-11-21)

The user supplied a screenshot of the **New scenario** dialog in the actual Etherlords II editor. It shows only **six selectable sizes: 20×20, 40×40, 80×80, 120×120, 160×160, 240×240**. The earlier speculative 56×56 and 72×72 sizes in the provisional table above **are invalid**; do not use them. The earlier 40×40 K1 recommendation is also rejected as far too small for the intended campaign.

**Agreed design preference:** default to **120×120**; use **80×80** only for purposefully compact chapters; **160×160** for substantial chapters; **240×240** only with good reason and enough meaningful content. Avoid 20×20 and 40×40 for regular campaign missions.

Updated provisional Kinet map plan:
- K1 — First Disturbance: **120×120**. Initial target **14–18 battle encounters**, **3 main questlines**, **3–4 side questlines**, pending pacing tests.
- K2 — The Frozen Passage: **120×120**.
- K3 — Echoes Beneath the Ice: **160×160**.
- K4 — The Silent Current: **160×160**.

Counts in the original table above are **superseded** and should be revisited because the larger maps need purposeful pacing, not simply more filler combat. These sizes and counts are current recommendations rather than final approvals.
